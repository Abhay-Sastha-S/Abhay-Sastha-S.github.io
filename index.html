Abhay Sastha S

I am an AI engineer at MyFi (TIFIN), where I build the agentic systems behind an AI wealth advisor for Indian retail investors.

I started in perception — computer vision and audio on small devices, where a model had to read a physical signal and make something happen. Language came next, and pulled that work back in: the table and chart extractors inside the multimodal RAG system I built were the same vision problem, now feeding an LLM, and audio returned the same way as a served TTS voice. The current work is agents — systems that decide what to do, not only what to say.

Each step has added either a modality or the ability to act, mostly one at a time. What I want to work on is the system that holds all of them at once: vision, audio, language and state going in, actions coming out, against a learned model of how the world responds. World models and robot policies are where that ends up, and it is the direction I am deliberately building toward.

Alongside this I research Mixture-of-Experts architectures, and I am finishing two undergraduate degrees in parallel — a B.Tech in Computer Science at VIT Vellore and a BS in Data Science at IIT Madras.

ExpertAttention: Output-Conditioned Aggregation for Mixture-of-Experts Transformers

Abhay Sastha S

Work in progress  ·  2026

An MoE router computes aggregation weights from the input before any expert has run, conflating which experts to activate with how to combine what they produce. ExpertAttention leaves routing untouched and replaces the fixed weighted sum with attention over the actual expert outputs, at under 0.003% compute overhead.
