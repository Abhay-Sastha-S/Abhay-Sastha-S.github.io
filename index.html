01.1 ⊕

ExpertAttention: Output-Conditioned Aggregation for Mixture-of-Experts Transformers

Abhay Sastha S

Work in progress  ·  2026

Sparse MoE transformers compute aggregation weights from the input before any expert runs, conflating two distinct decisions: which experts to activate, and how to combine what they produce. ExpertAttention (EA) leaves routing untouched and replaces the fixed weighted sum with a lightweight attention over actual expert outputs — Q = Wq·x, K = RMSNorm(Wk·E), V = Wv·E — at under 0.003% compute overhead.

At 84M parameters (k=2, 30K steps, C4) the work establishes three things. A cold-start failure: introducing EA from step 0 prevents expert specialisation entirely — router entropy froze at its 0.692 maximum against the baseline's healthy 0.637 → 0.501 curve, and the validation perplexity gap widened monotonically to +68%. A fix: a scheduled residual ramp — freeze to 10K, then λ decaying 1 → 0 over 8K steps, timed off the baseline's own specialisation transition — reaches val PPL 275.9 against 301.9 for the baseline and 323.8 for a hard switch. Linear and cosine ramps are indistinguishable, so the existence of a ramp matters and its shape does not. An independent signal: EA–router correlation traces an arc of −0.38 → 0.0 → +0.52, a learned policy the input-only router cannot express.

Next: integrating Expert Divergence Learning — EDL forces expert diversity, EA exploits it at aggregation time — and scaling to ≥1B active parameters on SlimPajama with downstream evaluation.
